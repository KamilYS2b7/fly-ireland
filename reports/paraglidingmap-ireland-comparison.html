Sources, method and limits

Read the public map as an anonymous visitor, using an initial broad scan and 28 overlapping detailed views. Compare names, named terrain, coordinates and available wind sectors against the 136 current plugin sites. Distance alone never decides a match. This is a comparison of observed public records, not a guarantee of the provider’s internal database completeness or current site access.

  • 75 missing-area candidates are catalogue records, not a claim of 75 independent, currently usable hills. Several records can describe launches in one mountain range.
  • 4 additional-launch candidates have different slope/wind exposure from nearby existing sites; exact take-off positions require local confirmation.
  • 11 uncertain counterparts include 9 map records and 2 entries visible only on the Ireland country page.
  • Public marker wind directions are coarse compass categories; do not interpret them as precise continuous degree limits.
  • Source closure labels and sector discrepancies are recorded as observed and have not been independently verified.
  • Geographical groups are approximate areas, not administrative boundaries or changes to plugin filters.
  • No plugin catalogue or behaviour was changed. Gortin remains intentionally excluded; Flagstaff was not found in the observed map records.
